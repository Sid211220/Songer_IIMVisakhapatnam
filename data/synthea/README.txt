Filtered output of Synthea (https://github.com/synthetichealth/synthea, Apache-2.0), run with
seed 20261020, population 1500 (Massachusetts default), CSV exporter. Reference date 2026-10-20.
Only the columns the twin needs are kept; names, addresses and identifiers were dropped.
Synthea patients are entirely synthetic. They follow Synthea's default US demographics; Prahari
adds an India-calibrated lifestyle (diet, routine) in the simulation layer.
