How Prahari works

1 · EHRSynthea record: age, sex, diagnosis, HbA1c, BMI, eGFR, BP, lipids, medicines, comorbidities, plus synthetic genetic risk.
2 · StreamsCGM every 5 min; watch heart rate, HRV (RMSSD), steps and sleep stages; meal photos/logs; insulin pen.
3 · TwinPersonal glucose-insulin model. Prior from the EHR, fitted in week 1, kept in sync with every CGM reading, re-fitted nightly.
4 · Hybrid AILightGBM uses the twin's forecast plus CGM, lifestyle and EHR features: glucose at 30-120 min and spike risk, with SHAP reasons.
5 · ClinicRisk-ranked patients, 2-hour forecast with uncertainty, reasons, and what-if answers a doctor can use in a consult.

The virtual patients live 21 days of an Indian routine (regional meals, tea with sugar, late dinners, walks, short nights, stressful work days). A richer hidden physiology generates their true glucose; the twin never sees those hidden parts, so its forecasts face real model mismatch.

Limits. All numbers on this page come from synthetic people. Real patients are less regular, so real-world error will be higher; published benchmarks on real CGM data are the right comparison for a clinical study, not these results. Prahari is a proof of concept, not a medical device.
